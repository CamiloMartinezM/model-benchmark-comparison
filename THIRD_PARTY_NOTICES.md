The repository's MIT license applies to original analysis code and original documentation. It does not replace licenses or terms that apply to benchmark datasets, source material, model names, or bundled third-party software.

## Benchmark data

The data consolidate published benchmark results and API pricing. Benchmark owners and providers retain their attribution and applicable rights. This project does not claim ownership of their benchmarks or offer an independent license for upstream datasets.

The [source catalog](docs/sources.md) and the Excel **Sources** sheet identify the source of each observation. The **Source records** sheet preserves numerical extraction details. Main sources include Datacurve, Terminal-Bench, Cognition, Artificial Analysis, ARC Prize, and model-provider documentation.

## Plotly

The interactive HTML includes Plotly.js from the installed Plotly distribution. Its embedded copyright notices remain intact. The distribution's MIT license is reproduced in [licenses/Plotly-LICENSE.txt](licenses/Plotly-LICENSE.txt).
